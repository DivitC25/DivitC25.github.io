Project 0

Camera Geometry & Perspective

01

Selfie: The Wrong Way vs. The Right Way

A close-up portrait distorts facial features due to wide-angle perspective — the nose appears oversized while the ears recede. Stepping back and zooming in keeps the face the same size but compresses depth, producing a much more natural portrait.

02

Architectural Perspective Compression

Zooming in on a distant building flattens the scene — background elements appear compressed together. Walking closer without zoom restores natural depth and perspective, even when the building occupies the same frame size.

03

The Dolly Zoom

The dolly zoom — also called the "Vertigo shot" — combines a physical camera move with an opposing zoom. The subject stays the same size while the background appears to warp and shift, creating a disorienting sense of depth change.

Divit Chopra · UC Berkeley
