Part 1

Selfie: The Wrong Way vs. The Right Way

Close up

Zoomed in while further

The close up image clearly has more facial depth (with different facial features appearing at distinct depths from the screen) as compared to the flatter and more 2D appearing zoomed while further image.

The reason for this difference in apparent depth is that image magnification is inversely proportional to distance. When a close up image is taken, the scale of the difference in closeness to the camera between the nose and the cheeks is much larger relative to the facial distance from the camera. This causes the nose to seem protruding and more steep compared to the surrounding cheek. On the other hand, when the zoomed image is taken at a distance, the scale of the difference in closeness to the camera between the nose and cheek is much smaller relative to the total facial distance from the camera. This flattens the image, making it have less feature depth.
